## 🌩️ About Vyomanant Academy

**Vyomanant Academy** is the learning and training division of **Vyomanant Technologies Private Limited**, focused on helping learners build practical, industry-ready technology skills.

We empower students, freshers, working professionals, and career-focused learners through **hands-on training**, **real-world labs**, **project-based learning**, and **career-focused mentoring** across modern and emerging technology domains.

Our programs are designed to help learners understand concepts clearly, build real solutions, work with industry tools, and confidently apply their skills in professional environments.

> **Practical learning. Real projects. Career-ready skills.**

---

## 💡 What We Do

Vyomanant Academy helps learners and organizations build skills through structured, practical, and outcome-driven training programs.

We focus on:

- **Hands-on Training** in AI, DevOps, Cloud, Data Analysis, AI/ML, Automation, Software Tools, and emerging technologies
- **Real-World Labs** designed from practical industry scenarios
- **Project-Based Learning** with end-to-end implementation use cases
- **AI-Assisted Learning** using modern AI tools for faster understanding, productivity, and problem-solving
- **Career Preparation Support** including Resume, LinkedIn, GitHub Portfolio, and Interview Guidance
- **Workshops & Bootcamps** for students, freshers, working professionals, and career-focused learners
- **Corporate Training Programs** for teams, enterprises, and institutions
